Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 • Contact: aethercore.ai.support@gmail.com

1. Zero Personal File Access Guarantee

AetherCore-AI is strictly a hardware telemetry and thermal management utility. Our desktop engine only reads local CPU temperature, core utilization, memory pressure, and active process names/PIDs. We never scan, read, upload, or access your personal documents, photos, downloads, keystrokes, or browser history.

2. Information We Collect for Cloud Accounts

When you create an AetherCore-AI account (via Email/Password or Google Sign-In), we collect only your name, email address, encrypted password hash, explicit hardware permission toggles, and subscription billing status. This data is stored securely on MongoDB Atlas with TLS encryption.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your login and deliver Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) security codes via email.
  • To manage your 7-day free trial and optional ₹99/month subscription status.
  • To respond to customer support requests sent through the in-app Support desk.

4. Account Deletion & 30-Day Recovery

You may delete your account at any time from the Account & Support tab inside the application. Deleted accounts enter a 30-day recovery window before permanent removal from our cloud database.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy questions or data removal requests, email us at aethercore.ai.support@gmail.com.
